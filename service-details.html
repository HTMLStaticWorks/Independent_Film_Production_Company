Synopsys & Vision

'The Silent Echo' explores the internal struggle of a retired clockmaker who discovers an ancient acoustic anomaly in a remote mountain village. The film is a study of solitude, time, and the weight of unspoken grief.

Directed by Julian Thorne, the production utilized custom-built anamorphic lenses to capture the claustrophobic beauty of the valley. Every frame was meticulously graded to evoke a sense of timeless melancholy.

Production Timeline

Pre-Production Dec 2024 - Feb 2025

Location scouting in Northern Italy and initial script revisions.

Principal Photography Mar 2025 - May 2025

Shot on location using RED V-RAPTOR 8K cameras.

Post-Production June 2025 - Present

Final editing, color grading, and orchestral scoring underway.
