Common Questions

Investor Intelligence

Transparent answers to your most frequent inquiries regarding cinematic investment, risk management, and the Lumina ecosystem.

Investing in Lumina Cinema involves providing capital for a slate of independent films. Your returns are tied to the commercial success of these projects across multiple revenue streams, including theatrical distribution, digital streaming, and international syndication rights. Unlike traditional stocks, this is a tangible asset investment in creative intellectual property.

Our entry-level "Foundations" tier begins at $25,000. For investors seeking a lead feature focus or studio-wide strategic involvement, we offer "Executive" and "Visionary" stakes ranging from $100,000 to $500,000+. Each tier offers unique profit-sharing percentages and production credits.

Recoupment typically begins once a film enters its active distribution phase. Under our "Tier 1 Recoupment" model, investors are prioritized to receive 110% of their initial principal from the first dollar of revenue before any net profits are calculated or distributed.

We maintain total transparency through audited production budgets and quarterly financial reporting. Additionally, we leverage state film tax credits and production incentives which provide a "soft floor" for capital protection, mitigating risk before the film even reaches the screen.
